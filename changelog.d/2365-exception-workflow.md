- **Groundwork for member-requested booking-policy exceptions (#2365).** When a
  booking trips an eligible *soft* policy — a minimum-stay rule or the adult-member
  hosting requirement — the club will soon be able to review a single, auditable
  member request instead of the member simply being turned away. This release lays
  the durable foundation for that flow: a booking change request can now carry the
  complete frozen proposal, the exact policy evidence it is asking an admin to
  allow, a required member message, and the extra outcomes (withdrawn, superseded)
  the review needs.

  Nothing in a club's day-to-day booking behaviour changes yet — the member-facing
  request screens, the Booking Officer approval action, and the reserved-capacity
  handling arrive in the follow-up work that builds on this foundation. Hard limits
  (whole-lodge capacity, payment, membership, past dates) are never part of this
  and remain firm refusals.
