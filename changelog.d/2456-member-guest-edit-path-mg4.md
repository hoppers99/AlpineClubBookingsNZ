- **Member guests now work everywhere a guest can be added, and everyone who
  ends up on somebody else's booking is told (#2309).** A member who books first
  and thinks of a friend afterwards no longer has to cancel and start again: the
  Guests section of the edit panel carries the same two buttons the booking
  wizard does — **+ Add Member Guest** and **+ Add Non-Member Guest** — and the
  first opens the same find box. Before saving, the new person's row says what
  saving will do: whether they will be emailed and asked, with their bed held
  until they answer, or simply told.

  There is no separate admin booking page in this system, so a booking officer
  reads the same panel with the same two buttons. Their add works to the club's
  rule rather than the member's: the person is added immediately and emailed to
  say so, and that email is not a courtesy tick an officer can untick. Officers
  can search the membership by name from that box even where members cannot,
  because an officer who can already browse the member list loses nothing by
  being made to leave the booking to do it — but only if their role actually
  carries membership access. A Booking Officer whose role deliberately does not
  gets the exact-email box instead, and every officer lookup is written to the
  audit log exactly as a member's is.

  The public booking-request pipeline is the quiet one this closes. When an
  officer linked one of a request's places to a real member account, that member
  was put on a stranger's booking with nothing recorded and nobody told. All
  three places the pipeline writes guests now record the officer who placed them
  and email the member. If the guest list is re-arranged between the quote and
  the approval, both people are told — the one who now has the place, and the
  one who quietly lost it.

  And the message that was missing entirely: when a request is called off before
  anybody answers it, when the club takes a member guest off a booking, or when
  a booking-request booking is re-arranged, that member now gets one email
  saying so. Previously they were simply left holding an earlier email that had
  stopped being true.

  Two things are written down rather than built, because the club chose them
  that way. Agreeing to a booking covers it however it later changes — a booker
  can move the dates or add nights and the member is not asked again and not
  told — and a member who never agreed is still subject to the ordinary limits
  on taking yourself off a booking, so sometimes they cannot and only the club
  can help. Both are now stated in the member guide and in the emails
  themselves, in place of a flat promise that self-removal always works.
