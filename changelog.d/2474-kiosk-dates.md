- **The lodge kiosk now always shows the club's day, whatever the tablet's own
  clock says (#2474).** The kiosk worked out "today" from the display device
  itself, so a tablet left on the wrong time zone — or simply set wrong, which
  is easy on a wall-mounted screen nobody administers — opened on the wrong
  night. A hut leader could be reading one night's arrivals while every check-in
  the kiosk saved belonged to a different one.

  The kiosk now reads today from the club's New Zealand calendar, the same day
  the bookings, roster and check-in records are kept in. The night it opens on,
  the highlighted day in the week strip and the **Today** button all agree with
  the club.

  A kiosk left running on the wall also moves onto the new day by itself when
  the club's midnight passes, so nobody has to reload it in the morning. A
  screen someone has flicked to another night stays on that night — only a
  kiosk still sitting on "today" follows the club over.

  Nothing an operator does changes, and no existing booking, roster or check-in
  is affected — this is only which night the kiosk shows. Fixing a lodge
  tablet's clock is no longer something the kiosk depends on.
