- **Bed moves now show exactly which nights will change before anything is saved
  (#2595).** From the bed board, choose one allocation night or every existing
  allocation night for that person on the booking, including nights outside the
  visible 31-night window. The review lists changed and unchanged nights,
  approval consequences, shared-double promotions, and any conflict.

  Confirmation is all-or-nothing. If relevant allocation or occupancy details
  changed after review, the dialog shows a refreshed preview and asks for
  confirmation again; it never silently reruns auto-allocation or creates
  missing nights.

  A pointer drag now always opens the bed nearest the chip being dragged. The
  floating summary card beside the pointer previously counted towards the drop
  calculation, so a longer wording could open the review dialog for the bed one
  row below the one the card had just named.
