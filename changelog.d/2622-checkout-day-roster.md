- **Guests who leave in the morning are on that day's chore roster (#2622).**
  A lodge day now runs midday to midday everywhere the system asks "who is here
  today", so the people packing up and driving home are exactly the people the
  strip-the-beds and shut-down chores can be given to. Until now the roster
  looked only at who was sleeping that night, so a changeover morning showed
  nobody and a hut leader could not build a roster for it at all.

  On the Admin → Lodge Operations → Roster page and on the lodge kiosk wizard,
  a changeover day now lists both groups at once: everyone leaving that morning
  carries a **Departing** badge, everyone arriving that evening an **Arriving**
  badge, and nobody carries both. Morning chores go to the leavers, evening
  chores to the arrivals. No times are recorded or shown anywhere — midday is
  simply how a lodge day is defined.

  Availability, pricing and bed allocation are untouched: a check-out date is
  still not an occupied night, so back-to-back bookings on the same bed work
  exactly as before. Changing a booking's dates keeps looking after any chore
  already placed on its check-out day — a suggested one is tidied away, and a
  confirmed one is left alone and reported to the operator rather than silently
  deleted.
