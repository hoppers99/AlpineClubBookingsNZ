- **Editing a booking that is already under way no longer charges a guest for
  the nights they are away (#2736).** A guest can be booked for some nights of a
  stay and not others — in on Friday, home on Saturday, back on Monday. When an
  officer or a member edited such a booking after it had started, the change was
  priced as though the guest were there for the whole run from their first night
  to their last, so the nights in the middle they had never booked were treated
  as theirs.

  Two of those edits moved real money the wrong way. Taking a guest off the rest
  of a booking part-way through, or pulling the check-out date back, both took
  the gap nights off the bill as well — so the club handed back money for nights
  it had never charged for, and in the worst case a guest who had genuinely slept
  at the lodge came off the booking owing nothing.

  An edit now prices, quotes and saves exactly the nights each guest holds. The
  gap stays a gap: it is not charged, it does not appear on the guest's nights,
  and it no longer takes a bed that somebody else could have had. Extending the
  check-out still adds the new nights normally.

  **Nothing changes for an ordinary, unbroken stay** — every price, every night
  and every capacity answer is exactly what it was, which is what makes this safe
  to apply to bookings already in the diary.

  **Bookings edited before this fix are left exactly as they are.** If a member
  was charged, or refunded, for gap nights under the old behaviour, that stands
  on the booking until somebody decides otherwise; it is a money question for the
  club, not something the software has quietly corrected in the background.
