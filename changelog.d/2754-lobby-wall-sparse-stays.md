- **The lobby wall now shows a guest on every morning they leave, and draws a
  broken stay as a broken bar (#2735).** A guest can be booked for some nights
  of a stay and not others — in on Friday, home on Saturday, back on Monday. On
  the Saturday morning, when they were still in the lodge until midday, the wall
  in the lobby had already dropped them from the board, and the bar beside their
  name ran straight through the Saturday night as though they had never gone.

  Both are fixed. They are listed on the wall on every morning they leave, their
  bar is now two bars with the empty night visible between them, and each bar
  says which day that part of the stay ends. The tonight/look-ahead panels mark
  them as leaving on the morning they go and arriving again on the night they
  come back — with the dates of that part of the stay beside it, so a panel can
  no longer say "leaving today" next to a check-out three days later. The
  arriving and departing counts on each day now count each of those movements
  rather than only the first and last of the whole booking. This is what the
  roster and the kiosk have shown since the previous release; the wall was the
  last surface still describing the stay as one unbroken block.

  Where a booking is booked out over the whole lodge with a gap in it, the
  blockout panel and the week strip now leave the free night open instead of
  painting "whole lodge booked" across a night nobody has booked, and the
  welcome panel counts the nights the group really holds.

  Nothing changes for an ordinary stay with no gap in it — every count, every
  name and every bar is exactly what it was.

  The privacy rule behind the wall is unchanged and was re-checked as part of
  this. Whether guests' names and phone numbers may appear on the lobby screen
  at all depends on a count of who holds a bed each night, and that count is now
  taken independently of who the wall lists, so widening the board cannot
  quietly change who gets named.

- **A group that had the lodge to itself is no longer named on the wall on the
  morning it leaves (#2735).** When a group has the whole lodge, the wall shows
  the group's name and its size and never lists the people in it. There was a
  gap in that: on a day when the group's only remaining connection to the board
  was its check-out morning, the wall did not recognise it as a whole-lodge group
  at all and printed every member's name. Those names appeared on no other day,
  so the departure morning was the one day they leaked. The wall now withholds
  them on that morning too — while still, correctly, not claiming the lodge is
  booked out on a night the group no longer holds.
