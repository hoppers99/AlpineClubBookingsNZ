- **Clubs can now choose whether a booking edited later gets the group discount
  on the nights it adds (#2770).** Until now, if your club runs a group discount,
  it always applied to nights added afterwards — extending a stay, or adding
  another guest — exactly as if those nights had been booked at the start. Some
  clubs would rather discount only the original booking.

  Under **Admin → Booking Policies → Group Discount** there is a new setting,
  **Apply to nights added after booking**. It is **ticked by default**, which is
  what your club already does, so nothing changes for anybody until an
  administrator deliberately unticks it. Untick it and anything added later is
  charged at the ordinary rate instead, and the member or officer editing the
  booking is told so in plain English beside the price, so a higher number never
  looks like a mistake.

  Nights that were already booked keep the price they were booked at in either
  case. Changing this setting never re-charges anyone for a night they have
  already paid for, never alters a booking nobody is editing, and never touches
  the price of a brand-new booking, a waitlist offer or an approved booking
  request — it only decides what a later edit adds. The setting travels with the
  rest of your club's configuration when you export or import it.

  One switch covers every way a booking can be edited, on purpose: the same
  night can no longer cost one amount on one edit screen and a different amount
  on another. That includes the promo-code box on the edit screen, which used to
  size its discount on group-discounted rates regardless of the setting — so at a
  club that unticks this, the promo figure now matches the price beside it.

  The note beside the price appears only when the discount would genuinely have
  applied to what you are adding. If the party is too small for the club's
  minimum, or the stay falls outside the seasons the discount covers, the price
  is the same either way and no note is shown, because there is nothing to
  explain.
