- **A member who owes their subscription can now be booked in by the club and
  pay for it themselves (#2779).** If your club stops unpaid members from
  booking, that has always stopped them *starting* a booking — and it has never
  stopped them paying for one you made for them. That was deliberate, but
  nothing on screen said so, so the route was easy to miss and easy to mistake
  for a bug.

  Book the stay on **Book on Behalf** and press **Save as Draft**. It now
  appears on the member's dashboard labelled "Saved for you by the club" with a
  **Review & pay** button, the booking page says the club saved it for them, and
  their unpaid-subscription banner tells them a booking already saved for them
  can still be opened and paid. Paying confirms the booking exactly as usual.
  Their subscription is a separate debt and is still owed and still chased.

  Two limits are now stated where you need them rather than left to be
  discovered. An unpaid draft is **deleted 72 hours after it is saved** — the
  deadline is shown to the member on both the dashboard and the page that takes
  the money, and the Save-as-Draft step warns you before you choose it. And a
  **$0 booking has nothing for the member to pay**, so confirm that one yourself
  instead: their dashboard card and the booking wizard's own banner now say so
  rather than offering them a **Review & pay** button that leads nowhere.

  **Saving a draft for a non-member owner does not work, and the screen now says
  so.** The wizard also books someone with no login at all. They have no
  dashboard to open and are not emailed about a draft, so a draft saved for them
  is a booking nobody can pay — and it is deleted after three days with the beds
  never held. Confirm those instead.

  The Subscription Lockout and Book on Behalf guides now describe the whole
  route as designed behaviour, so nobody "fixes" it away later, and the member
  guides (**Booking a stay**, **Changing or cancelling a booking**) describe it
  from the member's side, including the club-saved draft's own label and button
  and the three-day deadline.
