- **Members and lodge visitors can now report a maintenance issue, and officers
  have a queue to deal with them (#2780).** A new **Lodge Maintenance** module (on
  by default) gives members a "report an issue" card on their dashboard and a
  short, mobile-first form — say what needs fixing, answer the club's own
  questions, and attach a photo if you have one.

  Whoever looks after the lodge works from **Admin → Lodge Operations → Lodge
  Maintenance**: a queue of reported faults to triage and resolve, a bounded editor
  for the questions the form asks, printable QR signs, and settings for photos and
  how long they are kept. An officer is emailed when a new fault comes in.

  A club can also print a **QR sign** for each lodge so anyone there can report a
  fault by scanning it, without signing in. That public path is **off by default**
  and has to be switched on deliberately; each lodge's code is long, unguessable,
  rate-limited, replaceable at any time, and grants nothing but reporting a fault at
  that one lodge. Photos are kept for a configurable number of days (30 by default)
  and then removed automatically, while the report itself is kept.
