Member guests — the four emails

Mock data. Each block shows the HTML email as a member receives it, and (behind the toggle) the exact plain default body an admin can edit at Admin › Email messages.

Four in MG2, five across the epic. One member-guest-added template covers notify-only mode, admin adds and pipeline rows, distinguished by a single server-composed sentence — so an admin has one place to edit, not three. The fifth (a withdrawal notice) belongs to MG4.
Being asked is not a preference. Owner decision D-16: these emails ignore the per-action "notify the member" tick and ignore the member's own notification-category preferences — a member who muted a category would otherwise never be asked and would silently expire off the booking N days later. They are withheld by the per-booking No emails switch, and each withheld send is recorded on the booking's withheld list.

3 · What the member decided — to the person who made the booking

Template key member-guest-consent-outcome. One template covers approved, declined, expired, and "expired but still on the booking", because the outcome and its consequence are both composed server-side.

Nothing has changed on your booking — the bed that was being held for Priya is now theirs.

Your booking has been repriced. NZ$48.00 has been added to your account credit and will come off your next booking.

Variant C — the request lapsed

Priya Kaur did not answer in time

Hi Dave, your request to add Priya Kaur lapsed on Fri 7 Aug 2026 with no answer, and Priya has been taken off your booking at Silverpeak Lodge, 8–10 Aug 2026.

Your booking has been repriced. NZ$48.00 has been added to your account credit and will come off your next booking.

View this booking

Variant D — lapsed, but the club must act

Priya Kaur did not answer in time

Hi Dave, your request to add Priya Kaur lapsed on Fri 7 Aug 2026 with no answer.

Priya is still on the booking, because this booking was priced by hand and only the club can change it — the club will re-quote the request. The club has been told and will be in touch.

View this booking

Variant D is the honest one, and it is required. Owner decision D-15 lets the sweep settle money as account credit so an ordinary paid booking always expires cleanly — but a booking that is quote-priced, has only this one guest, is in a status that forbids changes, or has already started genuinely cannot be changed automatically. Those land on an admin exception list, and this email names the real remedy rather than a dead-end "ask the club".

5 · Two things worth knowing about editing these

  • The party listing is one block, not a row per guest. The email templating has no loops, so the whole "Everyone on this booking" block — heading included — is built by the server and dropped in as a single value. An admin can move the block or leave it out; they cannot reformat it guest by guest. That would need a template-engine change.
  • The consent link never goes in a subject line. It is classed as sensitive, and two separate checks refuse to render it into a subject.

6 · A bug we found next door, and are fixing here

The existing check-in reminder email's guest list is broken today: it joins all the first names together and then all the last names together, so a real member receives Ada, Bob, Cleo Lovelace, Smith, Jones. MG2 already edits that email (to stop it naming a guest whose consent is still pending), so the join is fixed in the same change rather than carried.
