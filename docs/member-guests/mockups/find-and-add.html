Adding another member as a guest

This is the part members actually touch: a + Add Member Guest button in the booking wizard, the box you use to find the person, and everything the booker is told while that person decides.

Fifteen panels, drawn in the app's own colours and type. Every name and price is invented; every word of copy is the real wording.

Signed off by the owner on 31 July 2026, with four answers recorded at the foot of this page. This file is the frozen record of what was agreed before MG3 was built — see issue #2308 for the thread.

Before you startWhat is already settled

Decisions you have already made — shown here as decided, not re-asked

  • Finding someone is by exact email address by default. The membership list is not browsable unless a club deliberately turns that on.
  • A club can turn on name search, and it ships off. When it is on, the find box becomes a name list that narrows as you type. Under-18s are excluded from that list unless a club separately opts them in.
  • Any active member can be resolved by their email address — including someone without a login, and including a child at a shared household address. Where several people share an address, you pick from a list.
  • A found member's full name shows straight away, before they have agreed.
  • A row in any list shows full name and age group only — no email, no town, no photo, no membership type.
  • Someone you have added can see the booking immediately, including the other guests' names, before they decide.
  • Their agreement covers the booking however it later changes — we do not ask again if you move the dates.
  • The usual removal limits apply to them too — they cannot always take themselves off once the booking is priced or paid.
  • Repeated refusals get logged for an admin to look at, never automatically blocked (your 31 July decision on #2388). Someone trying a few dates for a friend is the normal case and must not be stopped.

So what am I actually asking?

Only whether this looks and reads right. There are four questions at the bottom — on badge wording, on what happens to the find box when a club turns name search on, on where the panel opens, and on the exact sentences we tell a booker while someone is deciding. Each has a recommended answer, so “go with the recommendations” is a complete reply.

One of those four matters more than the rest: panel 12. Two of its four sentences exist only because of choices you made earlier, and they are blunt. I would rather you read them now than meet them in production.

The two sentences worth arguing about. “They can see this booking… before they decide” means a person you added by mistake sees who else is coming. “Their approval covers… any later changes” means you can move a July weekend to a September one and we will not ask them again. If either now reads wrong to you, say so — it is a change to the rule, not to this wording, and it is much cheaper to change today than after it ships.

13 When someone can't be added

Where: the find panel, after you press Add to booking

One sentence, and it is the same sentence whether their subscription is unpaid, their profile is incomplete, or they are already booked in somewhere else those nights. A booker who could tell those apart could map another member's movements by trying date after date.

Sam Whittaker Adult Change Add to booking
✕

This member can't be added to this booking right now.

If you think that's wrong, ask them directly, or contact the club.

What backs this up. The same wording alone isn't enough — somebody patient could still try date after date and learn things from the pattern of yes and no. Your 31 July decision closes that: the add path gets a per-person speed limit, the “no such member” answer is made to take the same time as the others, and a member who is repeatedly refused against the same person gets written into a list an admin can look at. Never an automatic block — someone hunting for a date that works for a friend is the normal case.

Signed offThe four answers, as given

Answered by the owner on 31 July 2026 on issue #2308. Recorded here so the reasoning stays attached to the drawings it was given against.

1 · What the waiting labels say in the booking wizard

Answer: warmer, name-bearing wording in the wizard only. The booking wizard says “Waiting for Sam to approve”, “Sam approved”, “Sam will be told”. The booking-detail page keeps the wording MG2 shipped — “Waiting for consent”, “Consented”, “Told, not asked”.

This was the option against the recommendation, taken deliberately: warmer wording at the moment of booking is worth carrying two vocabularies for one underlying thing. To stop those two drifting apart, they are not a forked copy — both come out of the single consent-badge function, which gained a third audience alongside member and admin, so no consent state can be added without all three being considered.

2 · What happens to the email box when a club turns name search on

Answer: one box that takes either. If what the member typed parses as an email address it resolves exactly, by the email path; otherwise it searches names. No mode switch and nothing extra to explain. Both paths are rate-limited and audited, so this adds no privacy surface that the two-box version did not already have.

3 · Where the find panel opens

Answer: inline, as drawn. It opens underneath the Guests heading, so the booker keeps sight of their existing guests and the guest limit. No dialog.

4 · The four sentences at the review step

Answer: say all four plainly, always visible while anyone is waiting. That includes the two that follow from the earlier D-11 and D-13 decisions — that a person who has been added can see the booking, including the other guests’ names, before they decide, and that their agreement covers the booking however it later changes. Neither underlying rule was reopened.

Amendments — 31 July 2026, after the build. Four panels were re-drawn to match what actually shipped. Every change is authorised by an answer recorded on this page; nothing here was softened, and nothing was changed that the owner did not decide.

  • Panels 11 and 12 — the waiting labels. Drawn with MG2’s wording (“Waiting for consent · expires 7 Aug”, “Consented”, “Told, not asked”) because they predate Decision 1. Now drawn with the warmer, name-bearing wizard wording that Decision 1 chose. The booking-detail page is unchanged and still uses MG2’s wording; both come from one function with two audiences, so there is only ever one place to change.
  • Panels 2, 4, 5 and 6–10 — the box label. Drawn as two separate boxes, “Find a member by email address” and “Find a member by name”. Decision 2 replaced that with one box that takes either, so the open-search panels now read “Find a member by name or email address”. The default (email-only) panels keep their original label, which is what ships.
  • Panel 11 — the sentence under a waiting guest. Drawn as “Sam has been emailed and their bed is held until they answer.” In the wizard that is not true yet: no booking exists, so nothing has been sent and no bed is held until the booker confirms. What ships is the same sentence in the correct tense — “Sam will be emailed when you confirm this booking, and their bed is held until they answer.” This is the one amendment that is a factual correction rather than a decision, and it is recorded here rather than folded in silently.
