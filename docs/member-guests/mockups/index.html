What is already decided, and shown here as decided

These came out of your ticks on the epic and on this issue. The mockups implement them; nothing here reopens them.

  • Ask first is the default (D-3); "just tell them" is an explicit opt-down.
  • The consent email carries the full party listing — every guest's name, no money (MG2-D-a).
  • A member who has not answered yet can already see the whole booking page (D-11) — so the card sits inside the real page, and the honest consequence is that they see other guests' names and prices too.
  • A booker can extend an approved stay without asking again (D-13).
  • The ordinary "take yourself off" blockers apply even to someone who never consented (D-14) — which is why section 4 of the member page exists.
  • The nightly sweep settles money as account credit so ordinary paid bookings lapse cleanly (D-15); only four genuinely stuck reasons reach the admin exception list.
  • Consent email ignores notification preferences but obeys the per-booking No-emails switch (D-16).
  • This release ships the settings card, not MG1 and not MG4 (D-17); the two name-search toggles never travel in config transfer (D-18).
  • Waiting period ships at 7 days, admin-configurable 1–60.

What we would like you to tick

Three copy/placement calls, and one genuine choice

Everything else in the pack follows from decisions you have already made. If all four are fine as drawn, one comment saying so is enough — we are holding the visible half of the build until then.

  • MG2-M-1 — where the settings card lives. Drawn under Admin › Bookings setup with the other booking-policy cards. The alternative is its own Admin › Member guests page in the sidebar, which costs a new admin route and its whole registry fan-out. Recommended: Bookings setup, as drawn.
  • MG2-M-2 — the badge wording. "Waiting for consent", "Consented", "Told, not asked", "Added by Jo Admin", "Said no — could not be removed", "Lapsed — could not be removed". These are what a member sees as well as an admin. Recommended: as drawn.
  • MG2-M-3 — the exception list as a filter, not a page. Drawn as two extra filter chips on the existing bookings list. A dedicated queue page was rejected for the same registry-fan-out reason as above and is available later if you want it. Recommended: filter, as drawn.
  • MG2-M-4 — does the settings card stay editable while the module is off? Drawn as yes, with a banner (State A on the admin page), so a club can configure the policy before switching the feature on. The alternative is read-only until the module is on, which is safer-looking but forces an awkward order. Recommended: editable with the banner, as drawn.
One thing to look at hard, because it is the one that cannot be undone quietly: the two name-search toggles on the admin settings card, and the warnings beside them. Both ship off. Turning the first on makes your membership list browsable by any member; turning the second on does the same for children's names. If the warning wording is not blunt enough, say so — that copy is the whole safety rail.

Mock data, static HTML, no behaviour. The server half of MG2 (state machine, expiry sweep, exclusions, delegate rule) is being built in parallel and does not wait on this; the visible half does.
